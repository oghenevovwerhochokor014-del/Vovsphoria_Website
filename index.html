PUPPIES

What Is a Puppy?

A puppy is a young dog, usually under one year old. Puppies are born small and helpless, and they gradually learn to walk, eat solid food, play, and interact with other dogs and humans.

Origin

All domestic dogs belong to the species Canis lupus familiaris. Dogs evolved from ancient wolf populations, and humans have bred them into many different sizes, shapes, and breeds.

What Do Puppies Eat?

Puppies need nutritious food specially made for growing dogs. Their food should provide protein, fats, vitamins, and minerals for healthy development.

Interesting Facts About Puppies

  • Puppies are born with their eyes and ear canals closed.
  • They spend a lot of time sleeping because their bodies are growing.
  • Puppies use play to develop coordination and learn social skills.
  • Their baby teeth eventually fall out and are replaced by adult teeth.
  • Early positive experiences can help puppies become confident adult dogs.

Why Do People Love Puppies?

Puppies are loved for their companionship, playful personalities, loyalty, and ability to form strong relationships with humans. With proper care, a puppy can grow into a healthy and happy adult dog.

BUNNIES

All About Bunnies

Bunnies are soft, gentle mammals known for their fur, long ears, powerful hind legs, and playful behaviour. They are intelligent animals that can learn to recognise people and develop strong bonds with their owners. Rabbits belong to the family Leporidae.

Origin

Domestic rabbits belong to the species Oryctolagus cuniculus. This species originated in southwestern Europe and parts of North Africa. Humans have bred rabbits for many generations, resulting in different breeds, sizes, colours, and coat types.

What Do Bunnies Eat?

Bunnies are herbivores, meaning they eat plant-based foods. Their diet needs plenty of fibre to support healthy digestion and their continuously growing teeth.

Important: Avoid feeding rabbits chocolate, onions, garlic, avocado, or other foods that may be harmful to them.

Interesting Facts About Bunnies

  • Rabbits have long ears that help them detect sounds.
  • Their powerful back legs allow them to jump and move quickly.
  • Rabbits use body language and sounds to communicate.
  • Rabbits are herbivores and naturally eat plant-based foods.
  • Rabbits are often most active around dusk and dawn.
  • A group of rabbits can be called a colony.
  • Rabbits can learn routines and recognise familiar people.

Why Do People Love Bunnies?

Bunnies are loved for their soft fur, gentle nature, playful personalities, and unique behaviours. They can make affectionate companions when they receive proper food, housing, exercise, and care.

KITTENS

All About Kittens

Kittens are young cats known for their playful behaviour, curiosity, soft fur, and adorable appearance. They are intelligent animals that can develop strong bonds with people.

Origin

Domestic cats belong to the species Felis catus. Domestic cats are descended from wildcat populations that lived in the Near East and surrounding regions. Humans have bred cats into many different breeds, sizes, colours, and coat patterns.

What Do Kittens Eat?

Kittens need nutritious food specially made for growing cats. Their food should provide sufficient protein, fats, vitamins, and minerals to support healthy growth and development.

Interesting Facts About Kittens

  • Kittens are born with their eyes closed.
  • Their eyes usually begin opening when they are around one to two weeks old.
  • Kittens spend a lot of time sleeping because their bodies are growing.
  • Baby teeth eventually fall out and are replaced by adult teeth.
  • Play helps kittens develop coordination and important hunting behaviours.
  • Kittens use sounds such as meows and purrs to communicate.
  • A kitten's whiskers help it sense objects and spaces around its face.
  • Kittens become more independent as they grow.

Why Do People Love Kittens?

Kittens are loved for their playful personalities, curiosity, soft fur, and affectionate behaviour. They can become wonderful companions as they grow into adult cats when they receive proper nutrition, healthcare, socialisation, and a safe environment.
